How Smart Claims works

The aim is to remove manual data entry and shorten the wait for straightforward claims, without removing safeguards.

1
OCR reads the bill

Merchant, date, invoice number and amount are extracted into the claim form.

2
Policy rules check the claim

Eligibility, amount limits, date rules, duplicate signals and confidence are evaluated.

3
Claim follows the right route

Clear low-risk claims may be auto-approved. Missing data can be corrected; ambiguous or suspicious claims go to a reviewer.

4
Wallet credit is confirmed

After approval and successful wallet posting, the claim status changes to Wallet credited.

AI is not the sole approver. The LLM can help interpret document fields; deterministic policy rules and risk controls govern the decision.
